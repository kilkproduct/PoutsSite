Privacy policy.

Last updated October 8, 2026.

The short version

Pouts processes the information needed to respond to bot commands. This website does not use analytics or advertising cookies.

Information the bot processes

When you use a command, the bot may process information Discord provides to it, such as:

  • server, channel, and user IDs involved in the command;
  • the command name and options you submit;
  • server settings that an administrator configures.

The bot only receives message content that Discord makes available to it. It does not need to read a server’s message history to respond to slash commands.

Information this website stores

The site uses your browser’s local storage to remember whether you dismissed the cookie notice. That preference stays in your browser. The site does not load analytics scripts, advertising pixels, or third-party font services; its fonts are served locally.

Discord and other services

Pouts operates through Discord. Information you submit through Discord is also subject to Discord’s own policies. If you follow a link to another service, that service’s privacy terms apply to your visit there.

Retention and deletion

Information should be kept only for as long as needed to operate the bot or meet legal obligations. To ask about or request deletion of information associated with you or your server, contact the project through its Discord community or repository.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the service changes. The revision date above indicates when it was last updated.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project through its Discord community or repository.
